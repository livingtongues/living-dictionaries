### 1.3 🔴 The blank entries list: cause found, and it is five rows of data

`entries_view_render_failed` fired 5 times tonight across 5 sessions and 3 anonymous visitors —
4 on `san-sebastian-del-monte-m`, 1 on `birhor`, every one of them
`https://svelte.dev/e/each_key_duplicate`, every one of them a **completely blank results area**.

The guard has been firing since 2026-08-02 and the last three reviews narrowed the suspect to a
nested keyed `{#each}` inside `ListEntry.svelte`. Tonight I closed it. Line 248:

```svelte
{#each first_sense.semantic_domains || [] as domain (domain)}
```

The key is the semantic-domain **string**. I opened the content database for the failing dictionary
and looked for a sense whose domain list repeats a value:

```
DUP  entry 7XtLEJMhPIqq2mifh88w  semantic_domains = ["1.5","1.5"]
797 senses scanned, 1 duplicate
```

`birhor` has two of them (`["3","3"]` and `["2","2"]`). One duplicated string in one sense throws
inside Svelte's keyed-each and `<svelte:boundary>` catches it — after the whole list has already been
discarded. The user sees an empty page.

**Blast radius, measured — I scanned all 1,315 dictionary content databases on the box:**

| Dictionary | Senses with a duplicated semantic domain |
|---|---|
| `birhor` | 2 |
| `tanacross` | 2 |
| `san-sebastian-del-monte-m` | 1 |
| **Total** | **5 senses across 3 dictionaries** |

*(164 files in that directory failed to open; all 164 are the per-dictionary `.history.db` companions,
which have no `senses` table. Benign.)*

So: three dictionaries out of 1,315, but for those three the entries list is **totally** broken for
**every** visitor, signed in or not. `tanacross` has not reported it yet only because nobody browsed
its list in the window.

**Recommendation — fix the render, then the data, in that order.** Keying a list by a user-supplied
string is the bug; the five rows are just today's instance of it. Key by index (or de-duplicate in
the derived value) at <File path="site/src/routes/[dictionaryId]/entries/list/ListEntry.svelte" line="248" />,
and separately clean the five senses. Line 224 (`Object.entries(sentence.text)` keyed by `bcp`) is
safe — object keys cannot repeat. Line 199 (keyed by `sense.id`) is safe.
