## How these strings were translated

Register was anchored on the **newest** rows in each locale (`home_v2.*`, `misc.out_of_service`),
not the oldest — several legacy rows are weak machine output (`ha` "Rayuwa Dictionaries", `or`
"Living ଅବିଧାନଗୁଡ଼ିକ") and copying them would have propagated the damage.

- **Brand term per locale** taken from `misc.LD_singular`: es *Diccionario Vivo*, fr *dictionnaire
  vivant*, zh *活字典*, ru *Живой Словарь*, he *מילון חי*, ar *القاموس الحي*, id/ms *Kamus Hidup*,
  bn *জীবন্ত অভিধান*, as *জীৱন্ত অভিধান*, hi *लिविंग शब्दकोश*, vi *Từ Điển Sống*, am *ሕያው መዝገበ ቃላት*,
  or *ଜୀବନ୍ତ ଅଭିଧାନ*. **de and ha keep the English brand**, matching their own existing rows.
- **Domain vocabulary reused, not reinvented** — `entry_field.speaker` for "speakers",
  `home_v2.stat_*` for audio/photos/videos/contributors, `dictionary.entries` for "entries",
  `contributors.managers` for "managers", `about.OSK` for the glossing-language/keyboard pair,
  `about.import_data` / `about.export_data` for the import-export sentence.
- **`{dictionary}` preserved verbatim** in all 19 `error.dictionary_unaffected` rows; it is the
  only token in the whole set, and it was placed where each language's syntax actually wants it
  (`or`/`as`/`bn` take a case suffix directly on the token, `am` uses የ{dictionary}).
- **UI role respected**: `error.browse_*` / `go_home` / `dictionary_home` are link buttons (short
  imperative), `page_not_found` / `entry_not_found` are `<h2>` titles, `not_found_explain` and
  `page_moved_explain` are the calm explanation paragraph, `misc.dict_reset_confirm` is a

  button reads as its continuation.

- zh-CN/zh-TW were written separately (not converted) — TW takes 影片/搜尋/裝置/檔案/建立 and the
