### Structure hiding in prose

- **`;` in English = distinct senses** (23 rows), e.g. `Play piano; test melon by
  pinching to see if ripe` → `doopóok'`. `,` inside a sense = synonymous glosses.
- **`/` in the Pomo cell = variant forms** (51 rows), e.g. `tthíya / baatthín`,
  `biip'ót' / biiq'útt' / bu'úch'`. Two rows use the word `OR` instead (529, 1248).
  A few are degenerate (`chhée / chhée`, `khól / khól` — identical both sides).
- **`,` in the Pomo cell** (47 rows) is ambiguous: variants (`daamà, daamaámo`)
  in short rows, real sentence commas in long rows.
- **Trailing parentheticals in English** (349 rows; 38 leading) carry: number
  tags `(Pl.)`/`(sg.)`, usage/domain notes `(as of wood)`, literal translations
  `(literally thing of the wild)`, editorial notes `(Note: Body part vs.
  direction)`, dialect labels `(Upper Lake dialect)`.
- **Leading parentheticals** are subject/argument clarifiers: `(he)'s shouting`,
  `(face) north`, `(they)'re buying food`.
- **Affix entries** marked with a hyphen/slashes: `chhé-`, `xá-`, `ná-`,
  `/-shóm/`, glossed with grammatical terminology (`interrogative demonstrative`,
  `anaphoric third peron`).
- **Source typos in the English** (`mvoe`, `ot ground`, `9as in`, `peron`,
  `symptopm`, `extre,e`, `Posion`, `was away` for "wash away", `givento`,

## Phase 1 ✅ — decisions (Jacob, 2026-07-25: all recommended options)

1. **Sentences → entries**, plus a private tag + a one-month cron to revisit.
2. **Merge by identical Pomo form**, one sense per distinct meaning.

   moves to the sense definition.

## Phase 1 ✅ — pipeline (`/tmp/eastern-pomo/`)

`stage.py` (raw → `staging.jsonl`) → `review.py` (→ 18 chunk files) →
`decisions.json` (every manual ruling, keyed by source row) → `apply.py`

deterministic**: two full runs produce a byte-identical `payload.json`.

### Every row was read
All 2,253 rows were read in 18 ordered chunks; `review.py` asserts chunk coverage
and `apply.py` asserts that every source row ends up either in an entry or on the
skip list. No sampling.

### Rules applied (all auditable in `decisions.json`)
- Whitespace/NFC normalization; leading/trailing ellipsis runs trimmed.
- **85 English typo fixes across 77 rows** (`bursh`→`brush`, `mvoe`→`move`,
  `Posion`→`Poison`, `To message`→`To massage`, `seal`→`steal`, …). Found by
  reading plus a full spell-check sweep of the final glosses/definitions.
- **Only 2 Eastern Pomo forms touched**: `khiiq̓ákh`→`khiiq'ákh` (lone U+0313
  normalized to the apostrophe the other 2,252 rows use) and `shoobOy`→`shoobóy`
  (placeholder capital, corroborated by rows 214/215/1826).
- Variants from `/`, ` OR `, ` & `, parentheses, and per-row comma rulings
  (6 rows = variants, 3 rows = sentence punctuation).
- `;` in the English splits senses; `, i.e. …` and descriptive trailing
  parentheticals become the sense definition; grammatical tags (`(sg.)`, `(Pl.)`,
  `(object)`) stay in the gloss.
- Merge by identical headword; senses deduplicated on a normalized word-set key
  plus a **lossless substring collapse** (a gloss contained verbatim in another
  is absorbed into the longer one).
- Deterministic uuid5 ids from the RAW headword (`entry:<lexeme>`,
  `sense:<lexeme>:<i>`); `citations` carry `row N` for every contributing row.
