Living Dictionaries — audio playback derivative: listen before you decide

60 real recordings pulled from production R2 across 16 dictionaries. ORIGINAL streams live from media.livingdictionaries.app (exactly what a visitor gets today). Every other button is a candidate, embedded in this file. Nothing has been changed in production.
What each candidate is
Two ladders. p1 → p5 hold the codec fixed (Opus 32 kbps mono) and add one processing step at a time — that ladder answers “what should we do to the audio”. c1 → c5 hold the processing fixed (mono + linear loudness + trim) and vary codec/bitrate — that ladder answers “how small can we go before it hurts”.
